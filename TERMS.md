Last updated 11 September 2026.

By using DeFier you agree to these terms. They are short because the product is
narrow: it reads public blockchain data and shows you what it means.

## What DeFier is

An informational tool. It reads public data from the Base blockchain and
computes figures about a wallet: what it holds, what it has earned, and how
that compares with having held the same assets.

## What DeFier is not

**It is not investment advice.** Nothing here is a recommendation to buy, sell,
deposit, withdraw, borrow or lend. We are not your broker, adviser or
fiduciary, and no figure on any screen is a suggestion about what to do next.
Decisions about your own money are yours.

**It is not a prediction.** The simulator solves the economics of a range under
assumptions that are printed next to the result. Assumptions are not forecasts,
and past results describe the past.

**It is not a custodian or an exchange.** No funds move through DeFier.

## Accuracy, and its limits

We work hard to be exact and we say so where we cannot be. Figures come from
public chain data and third party price and market feeds, and both can be
wrong, delayed or unavailable. Where coverage is partial the screen says so
rather than presenting a total as complete. What we measure, and what we
decline to claim, is documented at [What we measure](/docs/measurement).
